About HalfScrew

Version: 1.2.0

Developer: myaiplug

HalfScrew is a professional-grade audio time-stretching and pitch-shifting plugin designed for musicians, producers, and audio engineers.

Features

  • Time-stretch from 50% to 200% speed
  • Pitch shift ±12 semitones
  • Real-time processing with Tone.js
  • 3-band EQ with bypass
  • LUFS normalization
  • Wet/Dry mix control
  • High-quality MP3 export
